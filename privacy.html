iNag Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 26, 2026 · Last updated: May 26, 2026

iNag ("we", "our", "iNag") is a proximity-based networking app operated by Service Points Inc. ("Service Points"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the iNag iOS app, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights.

1. Who we are

Service Points Inc., based in Canada. Contact: inag-info@inag.ca.

2. Privacy by default

iNag is designed so other users see a dummy identity of your choosing first — never your real name, photo, email, or phone number — unless you explicitly choose to reveal those fields. Reveals are per-recipient, per-layer (Personal / Business / Social), and reversible at any time.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you give us

  • Account: email, optional phone number, password (stored hashed).
  • Dummy profile: the avatar, dummy first/last name, and short bio you set as your public identity.
  • Personal layer (optional): real first/last name, real photo, real phone, real email — only collected if you choose to add them, and only shared per-recipient when you reveal.
  • Business layer (optional): business name, title, category, business contact details, business card image.
  • Social layer (optional): handles for social platforms you choose to add.
  • Mode + intent settings: the modes you are open to (Individual / Party / Street / Business / Townhall / Conference) and intent criteria (e.g., "open to date", "new in town"). Used to match you with nearby compatible users.
  • Support inquiries: when you submit the in-app Support form, we send the form contents to our CRM (HubSpot — see Section 5).

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Proximity events: when iNag detects another iNag user near you over Bluetooth Low Energy, we record an interaction row (timestamp, approximate location at meet time, the modes both sides advertised). Used to power "Met At" history and proximity matching.
  • Activity events: Nag requests sent, accepted, declined, blocks, and reveals — needed to operate the app's core features.
  • Device telemetry: app version, iOS version, basic crash logs, and anonymous funnel events (e.g., "completed sign-up"). Used for product quality and onboarding-funnel analysis. Telemetry never includes your real name, real photo, real email, real phone, Nag of the Day text, or social handles.

3.3 What we do not collect

  • We do not access your contacts list.
  • We do not record audio or video.
  • We do not collect precise GPS continuously — only at the moment of a proximity event.
  • We do not sell personal information to data brokers.

4. How we use information

  • To operate the app: matching, request lifecycle, reveals, the My Naggers wallet, Nag of the Day, tag events, support.
  • To enforce safety: blocks, abuse detection, and content moderation.
  • To improve the product: anonymous funnel analytics and crash logs.
  • To communicate: account notifications, support replies, occasional product updates (you can opt out of marketing emails).

5. Service providers we use

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage, serverless functions). Hosts your account, profile, and activity data. supabase.com/privacy.
  • HubSpot (CRM and support inbox). We push an external-facing contact record (email, iNag ID, FunID, dummy name, subscription tier) to HubSpot so the support team can identify you when you contact us. Your real name and phone are only sent to HubSpot if you explicitly enable the "Sync identity with HubSpot CRM" toggle in Settings → Privacy (off by default). hubspot.com/data-privacy/privacy-policy.
  • Apple Push Notification Service for app notifications. Apple's terms apply.

6. Sharing with other users

The only personal information another iNag user sees is what you actively share with them — your dummy identity by default, plus any layers (Personal / Business / Social) you explicitly reveal to that specific person. Reveals are reversible at any time from the Connection Card. Blocking another user hides your data from them immediately.

7. Data retention

  • Active account data: kept while your account exists.
  • Deleted account: a request to delete your account permanently removes your profile, reveals, and activity within 30 days. Some anonymized logs and aggregated analytics may be retained.
  • Support tickets: retained in HubSpot for 24 months after the last message, then archived or deleted.

8. Your rights

You can:

  • Access and edit your profile data in the app (Settings → Profile).
  • Revoke any layer you've revealed to another user, at any time.
  • Block any user, which hides your data from them.
  • Delete your account by contacting inag-support@inag.ca.
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you by contacting inag-info@inag.ca.

9. Children

iNag is not intended for users under 17. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 17. If you believe a minor has signed up, contact inag-support@inag.ca and we will delete the account.

10. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. Data at rest in Supabase is encrypted. Access is restricted by row-level security policies so each user can only read data they're entitled to. We never log real names, real photos, real phone numbers, or Nag-of-the-Day content in our analytics or telemetry.

11. International transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States (Supabase, HubSpot) and Canada (operations). By using iNag you consent to this transfer.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be announced in the app or by email at least 14 days before they take effect.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy: inag-info@inag.ca
Support: inag-support@inag.ca
